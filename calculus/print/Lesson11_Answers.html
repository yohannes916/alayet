ANSWER KEY — Integration Lesson 11 Test: Motion I — From Velocity to Position

For the grader · full working shown · give credit for correct method with small arithmetic slips

1. A robot rolls at a constant 8 m/s from s = 0. Where is it after 12 seconds?
Work: displacement = ∫₀¹² 8 dt = 8t |₀¹² = 96 m. Position = 0 + 96 = 96. Answer: s = 96 meters.
2. A car passes mile marker 10 at a steady 50 mph. What marker after 2 hours?
Work: displacement = 50 × 2 = 100 miles. Position = starting marker + displacement = 10 + 100 = 110. (Forgetting the starting 10 → 100 is the classic error.) Answer: mile marker 110.
3. v(t) = 2t m/s; displacement from t = 0 to t = 5.
Work: ∫₀⁵ 2t dt = t² |₀⁵ = 25 − 0 = 25. (Antiderivative check: derivative of t² is 2t ✓.) Answer: 25 meters.
4. Start s = 2 m, v(t) = 3t² m/s; position at t = 2.
Work: displacement = ∫₀² 3t² dt = t³ |₀² = 8. Position = 2 + 8 = 10. (Check: derivative of t³ is 3t² ✓.) Answer: s = 10 meters.
5. Start s = 20 m, v = −3 m/s for 4 seconds. Where does it end up?
Work: displacement = ∫₀⁴ (−3) dt = −3 × 4 = −12 m (negative = backward). Position = 20 + (−12) = 8. Answer: s = 8 meters.
6. v = 5 m/s on [0, 2], then v = −2 m/s on [2, 6]: (a) displacement, (b) total distance.
Work: forward piece: 5 × 2 = +10 m. Backward piece: (−2) × 4 = −8 m (width 6 − 2 = 4 s). (a) Net = 10 − 8 = +2 m. (b) Total = 10 + 8 = 18 m. Answer: (a) +2 meters; (b) 18 meters.
7. v(t) = 8 − 2t: (a) when does it stop? (b) distance traveled by then.
Work: (a) v = 0: 8 − 2t = 0 → t = 4 s. (b) v stays positive before t = 4, so distance = displacement = ∫₀⁴ (8 − 2t) dt = (8t − t²) |₀⁴ = 32 − 16 = 16 m. (Check: derivative of 8t − t² is 8 − 2t ✓.) Answer: (a) t = 4 s; (b) 16 meters.
8. v(t) = 4 − 2t on [0, 4]: displacement and total distance.
Work: v = 0 at t = 2. Antiderivative: 4t − t². Forward piece ∫₀² = (8 − 4) − 0 = +4 m. Backward piece ∫₂⁴ = (16 − 16) − (8 − 4) = 0 − 4 = −4 m. Displacement = 4 − 4 = 0 (it returns to its start!). Total distance = 4 + 4 = 8 m. Answer: displacement 0; total distance 8 meters.
9. Graph: v = 6 flat for 3 s, then a line from 6 down to 0 over 2 s. Displacement by geometry.
Work: rectangle: 6 × 3 = 18 m. Triangle: ½ × 2 × 6 = 6 m. All above the axis, so both count positive. Displacement = 18 + 6 = 24 m. Answer: 24 meters.
10. CHALLENGE: v(t) = t² − 2t on [0, 3]: turnaround, net displacement, total distance.
Work: factor: v = t(t − 2), zero at t = 0 and t = 2; v is negative on (0, 2) (test t = 1: 1 − 2 = −1) and positive on (2, 3) (test t = 2.5: 6.25 − 5 = +1.25) → turnaround at t = 2. Antiderivative F(t) = t³/3 − t² (check: derivative is t² − 2t ✓). F(0) = 0; F(2) = 8/3 − 4 = −4/3; F(3) = 9 − 9 = 0. Piece [0, 2]: −4/3. Piece [2, 3]: 0 − (−4/3) = +4/3. Net = −4/3 + 4/3 = 0 — it ends exactly where it started. Total = 4/3 + 4/3 = 8/3 m. Answer: turns around at t = 2 s; net displacement 0; total distance 8/3 meters (≈ 2.67).
