ANSWER KEY — Integration Lesson 29 Test: Integral Properties & Symmetry

For the grader · full working shown · give credit for correct method with small arithmetic slips

1. Given ∫08 f dx = 15 and ∫03 f dx = 6, find ∫38 f dx.
Work: splitting rule: ∫08 = ∫03 + ∫38, so 15 = 6 + ∫38 f dx → ∫38 f dx = 15 − 6. Answer: 9.
2. Given ∫29 f dx = 4, find (a) ∫92 f dx and (b) ∫55 f dx.
Work: (a) reversal rule (swapped limits flip the sign): ∫92 f dx = −∫29 f dx = −4. (b) zero-width rule: equal limits → region of width 0 → area 0. Answer: (a) −4   (b) 0.
3. Given ∫16 f dx = 7 and ∫16 g dx = 2, find ∫16 (f + 4g) dx.
Work: linearity: ∫(f + 4g) = ∫f + 4·∫g = 7 + 4·2 = 7 + 8. Answer: 15.
4. Classify: (a) x⁶ + 3   (b) x⁵ − x   (c) x³ + 1.
Work: (a) f(−x) = (−x)⁶ + 3 = x⁶ + 3 = f(x) → even. (b) f(−x) = (−x)⁵ − (−x) = −x⁵ + x = −(x⁵ − x) = −f(x) → odd. (c) f(−x) = −x³ + 1; this is neither f(x) = x³ + 1 nor −f(x) = −x³ − 1 → neither (the constant +1 is even, the x³ is odd; the mix wrecks both symmetries). Answer: (a) even (b) odd (c) neither.
5. Evaluate ∫−33 x³ dx using symmetry, then verify with the FTC.
Work: x³ is odd and [−3, 3] is symmetric → integral = 0 (left area cancels right area). FTC check: antiderivative x⁴/4; at 3: 81/4; at −3: (−3)⁴/4 = 81/4; 81/4 − 81/4 = 0 ✓. Answer: 0.
6. Evaluate ∫−11 x² dx using the even shortcut, then verify by the FTC straight across.
Work: x² is even → 2·∫01 x² dx = 2·[x³/3]01 = 2·(1/3) = 2/3. Straight across: [x³/3]−11 = 1/3 − (−1/3) = 2/3 ✓. Answer: 2/3.
7. Evaluate ∫−22 (x⁵ + 3) dx by splitting into terms.
Work: x⁵ is odd on the symmetric interval [−2, 2] → contributes 0. The constant 3 makes a rectangle: 3 × (width 4) = 12. Total = 0 + 12. Answer: 12. (Full-FTC check: [x⁶/6 + 3x]−22 = (32/3 + 6) − (32/3 − 6) = 12 ✓.)
8. g is EVEN and ∫05 g dx = 9. Find (a) ∫−55 g dx and (b) ∫−50 g dx.
Work: (a) even shortcut: 2·9 = 18. (b) the left half mirrors the right half exactly, so ∫−50 g dx = ∫05 g dx = 9. (Consistency: 9 + 9 = 18 ✓.) Answer: (a) 18   (b) 9.
9. f is ODD and ∫04 f dx = 3. Find (a) ∫−44 f dx, (b) ∫−40 f dx, (c) the average value of f on [−4, 4].
Work: (a) odd on a symmetric interval → 0. (b) splitting: 0 = ∫−40 + ∫04 = ∫−40 + 3 → ∫−40 f dx = −3 (the left half is the negative mirror of the right half). (c) average = integral ÷ width = 0 ÷ 8 = 0. Answer: (a) 0   (b) −3   (c) 0.
10. A student writes: "∫−22 x³ dx = 2·∫02 x³ dx = 2·4 = 8." Explain the error, give the correct value, verify by FTC.
Work: the "double the right half" shortcut is only for EVEN functions. x³ is ODD, so the left half is the negative of the right half and they cancel. Correct value: 0. FTC check: [x⁴/4]−22 = 16/4 − 16/4 = 4 − 4 = 0 ✓. (The student's ∫02 x³ dx = 4 was correct; only the doubling was illegal.) Answer: correct value is 0.
