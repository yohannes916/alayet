ANSWER KEY — Integration Lesson 8 Test: FTC Practice, Polynomial Fluency

For the grader · full working shown · give credit for correct method with small arithmetic slips · fraction answers must be exact (equivalent forms OK)

1. Evaluate ∫04 x dx.
Work: F(x) = x2/2 (check: derivative is x ✓). F(4) = 16/2 = 8; F(0) = 0. Answer: 8 − 0. Answer: 8.
2. Evaluate ∫02 x2 dx.
Work: F(x) = x3/3 (check: derivative is x2 ✓). F(2) = 8/3; F(0) = 0. Answer: 8/3. (Classic error: x3/2 — dividing by the OLD power — gives 4, wrong.)
3. Evaluate ∫05 3 dx, then check with a rectangle picture.
Work: F(x) = 3x. F(5) − F(0) = 15 − 0 = 15. Picture check: region under y = 3 from 0 to 5 is a rectangle, width 5 × height 3 = 15 ✓. Answer: 15.
4. Evaluate ∫02 (2x + 5) dx.
Work: term by term: 2x → x2, 5 → 5x, so F(x) = x2 + 5x (check: derivative is 2x + 5 ✓). F(2) = 4 + 10 = 14; F(0) = 0. Answer: 14. (Classic error: dropping the constant term — forgetting 5 → 5x gives just 4, wrong.)
5. Evaluate ∫01 x3 dx.
Work: F(x) = x4/4 — raise to power 4, divide by the NEW power 4 (check: derivative is 4x3/4 = x3 ✓). F(1) = 1/4; F(0) = 0. Answer: 1/4. (Classic error: x4/3 → 1/3, wrong.)
6. Evaluate ∫12 x2 dx.
Work: F(x) = x3/3. F(2) = 8/3; F(1) = 1/3. Subtract: 8/3 − 1/3 = 7/3. Answer: 7/3. (Classic error: forgetting to subtract F(1) gives 8/3, wrong — the bottom limit isn't 0 here.)
7. Evaluate ∫13 (2x + 1) dx.
Work: F(x) = x2 + x (check: derivative is 2x + 1 ✓). F(3) = 9 + 3 = 12; F(1) = 1 + 1 = 2. Subtract: 12 − (2) = 10. Answer: 10.
8. Evaluate ∫01 (6x2 + 2x) dx.
Work: 6x2 → 6·x3/3 = 2x3; 2x → x2. F(x) = 2x3 + x2 (check: derivative is 6x2 + 2x ✓). F(1) = 2 + 1 = 3; F(0) = 0. Answer: 3.
9. Evaluate ∫12 (3x2 − 2x) dx.
Work: F(x) = x3 − x2 (check: derivative is 3x2 − 2x ✓). F(2) = 8 − 4 = 4; F(1) = 1 − 1 = 0. Subtract: 4 − (0) = 4. Answer: 4. (F(1) happens to be 0, but students must still show the subtraction — the question asks for it. Writing F(a) in parentheses is the habit being graded.)
10. Evaluate ∫02 (x3 − x + 2) dx, with a written derivative check.
Work: F(x) = x4/4 − x2/2 + 2x. Required check: derivative is 4x3/4 − 2x/2 + 2 = x3 − x + 2 ✓. F(2) = 16/4 − 4/2 + 4 = 4 − 2 + 4 = 6; F(0) = 0. Answer: 6. (Classic errors to watch: dropping the +2 term, or a sign slip on −x2/2. Deduct half if the "check ✓" step is missing — it was part of the question.)
