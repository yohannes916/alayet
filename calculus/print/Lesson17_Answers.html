ANSWER KEY — Integration Lesson 17 Test: Substitution I (Linear Insides)

For the grader · full working shown · give credit for correct method with small arithmetic slips · missing + C costs half · the classic error is forgetting to divide by the inside's slope

1. Find ∫(x+3)⁴ dx.
Work: inside x+3 has slope 1. Integrate as if the inside were one letter: (x+3)⁵/5, then divide by 1 (no change). Check: 5(x+3)⁴·1/5 = (x+3)⁴ ✓. Answer: (x+3)⁵/5 + C.
2. Find ∫(2x+5)² dx, and check by differentiating.
Work: as-if-one-letter: (2x+5)³/3; inside slope is 2, so divide by 2 → (2x+5)³/6. Check: 3(2x+5)²·2/6 = (2x+5)² ✓. Answer: (2x+5)³/6 + C.
3. Find ∫e^(3x) dx.
Work: as-if-one-letter: e^(3x); divide by the slope 3. Check: (e^(3x)/3)′ = 3e^(3x)/3 = e^(3x) ✓. Answer: e^(3x)/3 + C.
4. Find ∫cos(4x) dx.
Work: ∫cos gives sin (no minus); divide by the slope 4: sin(4x)/4. Check: 4cos(4x)/4 = cos(4x) ✓. Answer: sin(4x)/4 + C.
5. Find ∫sin(3x) dx.
Work: ∫sin gives −cos (the Lesson 16 minus); divide by the slope 3: −cos(3x)/3. Check: (−cos(3x)/3)′ = −(−sin(3x))·3/3 = sin(3x) ✓. Answer: −cos(3x)/3 + C. (Watch for +cos(3x)/3 or −cos(3x) without the /3.)
6. A student writes ∫e^(5x) dx = e^(5x) + C. Show the mistake, then correct it.
Work: differentiate the claim: (e^(5x))′ = 5e^(5x) — five times too big, because the chain rule multiplies by the inside's slope 5. Fix: divide by 5. Verify: (e^(5x)/5)′ = 5e^(5x)/5 = e^(5x) ✓. Answer: the student forgot to divide by 5; correct answer e^(5x)/5 + C.
7. Compute ∫₀¹ (6x+2)² dx.
Work: antiderivative: (6x+2)³/3 ÷ 6 = (6x+2)³/18. (Check: 3(6x+2)²·6/18 = (6x+2)² ✓.) Inside at the limits: 6·1+2 = 8 and 6·0+2 = 2. FTC: 8³/18 − 2³/18 = 512/18 − 8/18 = 504/18 = 28. Answer: 28.
8. Compute ∫₀^π cos(x/2) dx.
Work: inside x/2 has slope ½; dividing by ½ = multiplying by 2, so the antiderivative is 2 sin(x/2). (Check: 2cos(x/2)·½ = cos(x/2) ✓.) FTC: [2 sin(x/2)]₀^π = 2 sin(π/2) − 2 sin 0 = 2·1 − 0 = 2. Answer: 2.
9. Compute ∫₀^π sin(2x) dx, and explain via signed area.
Work: antiderivative: −cos(2x)/2. FTC: [−cos(2x)/2]₀^π = (−cos 2π)/2 − (−cos 0)/2 = (−1)/2 − (−1)/2 = −½ + ½ = 0. Explanation: sin(2x) runs at double speed, so on [0, π] it completes one FULL cycle — a hump above the axis (0 to π/2) and a matching hump below (π/2 to π) that cancel exactly. Answer: 0 — a full cycle's areas above and below the axis cancel.
10. Challenge: compute ∫₀¹ (e^(4x) + (3x+2)²) dx exactly.
Work: term by term. ∫e^(4x) dx = e^(4x)/4; ∫(3x+2)² dx = (3x+2)³/3 ÷ 3 = (3x+2)³/9. (Checks: 4e^(4x)/4 = e^(4x) ✓; 3(3x+2)²·3/9 = (3x+2)² ✓.) FTC with F(x) = e^(4x)/4 + (3x+2)³/9. Top x = 1: e⁴/4 + 5³/9 = e⁴/4 + 125/9. Bottom x = 0: e⁰/4 + 2³/9 = 1/4 + 8/9. Subtract: (e⁴ − 1)/4 + 117/9 = (e⁴ − 1)/4 + 13. Answer: (e⁴ − 1)/4 + 13 (≈ 26.4).
