ANSWER KEY — Integration Lesson 6 Test: The Definite Integral, the Official Symbol

For the grader · full working shown · give credit for correct method with small arithmetic slips

1. Rain falls at a rate of r(t) millimeters per hour. Write the definite integral that means "the total rain that fell from hour 1 to hour 5."
Work: start = 1 (lower limit), end = 5 (upper limit), rate = r(t), variable is t so the slice width is dt. Answer: ∫15 r(t) dt.
2. In the integral ∫08 (3x + 2) dx, identify: (a) the integrand, (b) the lower limit, (c) the upper limit, and (d) what the dx stands for.
Work: the integrand is the function between the ∫ and the dx; the limits are the start/end values on the x-axis. Answer: (a) 3x + 2; (b) 0; (c) 8; (d) dx is "a tiny little bit of x" — the infinitely skinny width of each slice.
3. Evaluate ∫15 6 dx using geometry. Name the shape you used.
Work: constant integrand → rectangle. Width = 5 − 1 = 4, height = 6. Area = 6 × 4 = 24. Answer: 24 (a rectangle).
4. Evaluate ∫03 4x dx using geometry. Name the shape you used.
Work: the line 4x rises from height 0 (at x = 0) to height 4 × 3 = 12 (at x = 3) → triangle with base 3, height 12. Area = ½ × 3 × 12 = 18. Answer: 18 (a triangle).
5. Evaluate ∫55 (x³ + 9) dx, and explain in one sentence why no computing is needed.
Work: the region runs from x = 5 to x = 5 — width 5 − 5 = 0, so it encloses no area regardless of the integrand. Answer: 0, because a zero-width region has zero area (∫aa f dx = 0 always).
6. Evaluate ∫04 (x + 2) dx using geometry.
Work: heights at the ends: at x = 0, height = 2; at x = 4, height = 6. Trapezoid: ½(2 + 6) × 4 = ½ × 8 × 4 = 16. (Or rectangle 2 × 4 = 8 plus triangle ½ × 4 × 4 = 8 → 16.) Answer: 16.
7. A car's speed after t hours is v(t) miles per hour. Translate ∫26 v(t) dt into a plain-English sentence, including the units of the answer.
Work: limits 2 → 6 hours; integrand is a speed, so each slice v(t) × dt is a tiny distance; the sum is total distance. Units: (miles/hour) × hours = miles. Answer: "the total distance, in miles, the car travels between t = 2 and t = 6 hours."
8. Given ∫03 f(x) dx = 8 and ∫39 f(x) dx = 11. Find ∫09 f(x) dx, and name the property.
Work: the pieces 0→3 and 3→9 fit together with no gap or overlap, so ∫09 f = ∫03 f + ∫39 f = 8 + 11 = 19. Answer: 19, by the splitting property (chop the region at x = 3).
9. Given ∫012 g(x) dx = 40 and ∫05 g(x) dx = 15. Find ∫512 g(x) dx.
Work: whole = left + right: 40 = 15 + ∫512 g, so ∫512 g = 40 − 15 = 25. Answer: 25.
10. Flow rate climbs along a straight line from 0 (at x = 0) to 6 (at x = 3), then holds steady at 6 from x = 3 to x = 5. Evaluate ∫05 f(x) dx with units.
Work: split at x = 3. Piece 1 (ramp): triangle with base 3, height 6 → ½ × 3 × 6 = 9. Piece 2 (flat): rectangle, width 5 − 3 = 2, height 6 → 12. Total = 9 + 12 = 21. Units: (liters/minute) × minutes = liters. Answer: 21 liters — the total water delivered in the 5 minutes.
