ANSWER KEY — Integration Lesson 2 Test: Area Under a Graph = the Total

For the grader · full working shown · give credit for correct method with small arithmetic slips

1. A hose fills a barrel at a steady 7 liters per minute for 6 minutes. What is the area of the rectangle under the flow-rate graph, and how much water is in the barrel?
Work: rectangle: width 6 min, height 7 L/min → area = 7 × 6 = 42. Units: (L/min) × min = L. Area under a rate graph = the total. Answer: area = 42, meaning 42 liters of water.
2. A van drives at a steady 45 miles per hour from t = 2 hours to t = 6 hours. How far does it travel during that stretch?
Work: width = 6 − 2 = 4 hours (NOT 6). Distance = 45 × 4 = 180. Answer: 180 miles. (Classic error: using width 6 → 270 miles, wrong — the stretch starts at t = 2, not t = 0.)
3. A bicycle starts from rest and speeds up steadily to 12 m/s over 6 seconds. How far does it travel?
Work: slanted line from 0 → triangle. Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 6 × 12 = 36. Answer: 36 meters. (Classic error: 12 × 6 = 72 forgets the ½ — the bike was only at 12 m/s for the last instant.)
4. A ball is dropped; its speed grows steadily from 0 to 20 m/s during the first 2 seconds. How far does it fall?
Work: triangle: base 2 s, height 20 m/s → ½ × 2 × 20 = 20. Answer: 20 meters.
5. A scooter already moving at 5 m/s speeds up steadily to 15 m/s over 4 seconds. Find the distance via rectangle + triangle.
Work: rectangle (starting speed held the whole time): 5 × 4 = 20 m. Triangle (extra speed grows 0 → 15 − 5 = 10): ½ × 4 × 10 = 20 m. Total = 20 + 20 = 40. Answer: 40 meters. (Watch for triangle height 15 instead of 10 — the triangle only covers the EXTRA speed above 5.)
6. Rainfall rate steady at 3 mm/hour for 5 hours. Find the area, its unit, and its meaning.
Work: area = 3 × 5 = 15. Unit: (mm/hour) × hours = mm — the hours cancel. Meaning: the total depth of rain that fell, i.e. what a rain gauge would read. Answer: 15 millimeters of total rainfall.
7. A race car accelerates from rest with a straight-line speed graph and covers 48 meters in 8 seconds. What top speed does it reach?
Work: triangle with known area, unknown height: ½ × 8 × h = 48 → 4h = 48 → h = 12. Check: ½ × 8 × 12 = 48 ✓. Answer: 12 m/s.
8. Earning rate climbs steadily from $10/day to $20/day over 5 days. Total earned?
Work: rectangle: 10 × 5 = $50. Triangle: extra rate grows 0 → (20 − 10) = 10, so ½ × 5 × 10 = $25. Total = 50 + 25 = 75. Answer: $75.
9. A car traveling 24 m/s brakes steadily to a stop over 4 seconds. How far does it travel while stopping?
Work: downhill slanted line from 24 to 0 → still a triangle, base 4, height 24 (height at the start). Distance = ½ × 4 × 24 = 48. Answer: 48 meters. (Common mistake: thinking a braking car covers no distance — it moves the whole time it slows.)
10. Tram: 8 m/s for 5 s, then steadily 8 → 16 m/s over 4 s, then 16 m/s for 10 s. Total distance?
Work: piece 1 (rectangle): 8 × 5 = 40 m. Piece 2 (trapezoid = rectangle + triangle): 8 × 4 = 32 m plus ½ × 4 × (16 − 8) = 16 m → 48 m. Piece 3 (rectangle): 16 × 10 = 160 m. Total = 40 + 48 + 160 = 248. Answer: 248 meters.
