ANSWER KEY — Integration Lesson 20 Test: Capstone, Putting It All Together

For the grader · full working shown · give credit for correct method with small arithmetic slips

1. (adding rate × time pieces) You bike at 10 mph for 2 hours, then at 14 mph for 1.5 hours. What is your total distance?
Work: chop–multiply–add (Lesson 1): 10 × 2 = 20 miles; 14 × 1.5 = 21 miles; total = 20 + 21. Answer: 41 miles.
2. (area under a graph) A train's speed rises in a straight line from 4 m/s to 10 m/s over 6 seconds. Find the distance by geometry.
Work: distance = area under the speed graph (Lesson 2). Trapezoid: ½ × (4 + 10) × 6 = ½ × 14 × 6 = 42. (Or: rectangle 4 × 6 = 24 plus triangle ½ × 6 × 6 = 18; 24 + 18 = 42 ✓.) Answer: 42 meters.
3. (Fundamental Theorem) Evaluate ∫ from 1 to 3 of (2x + 1) dx.
Work: antiderivative F(x) = x² + x. FTC: F(3) − F(1) = (9 + 3) − (1 + 1) = 12 − 2. Answer: 10.
4. (signed area: net vs total) v(t) = 4 − 2t m/s for 0 ≤ t ≤ 4. Find (a) net displacement and (b) total distance.
Work: (a) net = ∫₀⁴ (4 − 2t) dt = [4t − t²]₀⁴ = 16 − 16 = 0 m. (b) v = 0 at t = 2. Forward: ∫₀² = [4t − t²]₀² = 8 − 4 = 4 m. Backward: ∫₂⁴ = 0 − 4 = −4 m, i.e. 4 m backward. Total = 4 + 4 = 8. Answer: (a) net = 0 m (it returns to its start) · (b) total = 8 m.
5. (area between curves) Find the area between y = x and y = x² (crossings at x = 0 and 1).
Work: on [0, 1] the line is on top (at x = ½: ½ vs ¼). Area = ∫₀¹ (x − x²) dx = [x²/2 − x³/3]₀¹ = 1/2 − 1/3 = 3/6 − 2/6. Answer: 1/6 square unit.
6. (gravity, g = 10) A ball is thrown straight up from the ground at 30 m/s. When is the peak, and how high?
Work: v(t) = 30 − 10t = 0 → t = 3 s. Height = ∫₀³ (30 − 10t) dt = [30t − 5t²]₀³ = 90 − 45 = 45. Answer: peak at t = 3 seconds, 45 meters high.
7. (average value) Find the average value of f(x) = x² on [0, 3].
Work: average = (1/(b − a)) ∫ₐᵇ f = (1/3) ∫₀³ x² dx = (1/3) × [x³/3]₀³ = (1/3) × 9 = 3. Answer: 3. (Sensible: f runs from 0 to 9; the average leans low because x² spends more time small.)
8. (e^x and 1/x) (a) ∫ from 0 to 1 of e^x dx and (b) ∫ from 1 to e of (2/x) dx.
Work: (a) [e^x]₀¹ = e − 1 ≈ 2.718 − 1 = 1.718 ≈ 1.72. (b) [2 ln x]₁^e = 2 ln e − 2 ln 1 = 2(1) − 2(0) = 2. Answer: (a) e − 1 ≈ 1.72 · (b) 2.
9. (linear inside) Evaluate ∫ from 0 to π/4 of cos(2x) dx.
Work: linear inside (a = 2): antiderivative = sin(2x)/2. Evaluate: sin(π/2)/2 − sin(0)/2 = 1/2 − 0. Answer: 1/2. (Common error: forgetting the ÷2 gives 1, wrong.)
10. (u-substitution) Evaluate ∫ from 0 to 1 of 2x(x² + 1)³ dx.
Work: u = x² + 1, du = 2x dx — the 2x dx is exactly present. Limits: x = 0 → u = 1; x = 1 → u = 2. Integral = ∫₁² u³ du = [u⁴/4]₁² = (16 − 1)/4 = 15/4. Answer: 15/4 = 3.75.
