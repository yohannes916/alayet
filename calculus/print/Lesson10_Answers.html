ANSWER KEY — Integration Lesson 10 Test: Area Between Two Curves

For the grader · full working shown · give credit for correct method with small arithmetic slips

1. Area between y = 9 and y = 4 from x = 0 to x = 6, checked with the rectangle formula.
Work: top = 9, bottom = 4, gap = 5. ∫₀⁶ 5 dx = 5x |₀⁶ = 30 − 0 = 30. Rectangle check: width 6 × height 5 = 30 ✓. Answer: 30 square units.
2. Area between y = x + 7 and y = x + 3 from x = 0 to x = 5.
Work: parallel lines; top is y = x + 7 (test x = 0: 7 > 3). Gap = (x + 7) − (x + 3) = 4 — the x's cancel. ∫₀⁵ 4 dx = 4x |₀⁵ = 20. Answer: 20 square units.
3. Area between y = 10 and y = 2x from x = 0 to x = 4.
Work: top test at x = 4 (the biggest x here): 10 vs 2 × 4 = 8, so y = 10 stays on top on the whole interval. ∫₀⁴ (10 − 2x) dx = (10x − x²) |₀⁴ = (40 − 16) − 0 = 24. (Check: derivative of 10x − x² is 10 − 2x ✓.) Answer: 24 square units.
4. Which is on top between 0 and 1: y = x² or y = x³?
Work: test x = ½: x² gives ¼ = 0.25; x³ gives ⅛ = 0.125. Since 0.25 > 0.125, the square is on top — multiplying a number less than 1 by itself again makes it even smaller, so higher powers sit lower on (0, 1). Answer: y = x² is on top (0.25 > 0.125 at x = ½).
5. Area between y = 9 and y = x² from x = 0 to x = 3.
Work: top test at x = 1: 9 vs 1 → line on top (they only meet at x = 3, since 3² = 9). ∫₀³ (9 − x²) dx = (9x − x³/3) |₀³ = (27 − 9) − 0 = 18. (Check: derivative of 9x − x³/3 is 9 − x² ✓.) Answer: 18 square units.
6. Area of the region enclosed by y = 3x and y = x².
Work: crossings: 3x = x² → x² − 3x = 0 → x(x − 3) = 0 → x = 0 and x = 3. Top test at x = 1: line → 3, parabola → 1 → line on top. ∫₀³ (3x − x²) dx = (3x²/2 − x³/3) |₀³ = (27/2 − 9) − 0 = 27/2 − 18/2 = 9/2. Answer: 9/2 square units (= 4.5).
7. Runner A at 7 m/s, runner B at 4 m/s; A's lead after 20 seconds.
Work: the lead is the area between the two speed graphs. A's graph (height 7) is on top. Lead = ∫₀²⁰ (7 − 4) dt = ∫₀²⁰ 3 dt = 3t |₀²⁰ = 60. Check: A ran 140 m, B ran 80 m, difference 60 ✓. Answer: 60 meters.
8. Faucet A at 8 L/min, faucet B at 2t L/min; when are the rates equal, and how far ahead is A then?
Work: rates equal: 8 = 2t → t = 4 minutes. A on top before then (test t = 1: 8 vs 2). Extra water = ∫₀⁴ (8 − 2t) dt = (8t − t²) |₀⁴ = 32 − 16 = 16. Check: A delivered 32 L, B delivered t² |₀⁴ = 16 L, difference 16 ✓. Answer: rates equal at t = 4 min; A is 16 liters ahead.
9. Revenue rate 20 $/day, cost rate 8 + 2t $/day; total profit from t = 0 to t = 6.
Work: profit rate = 20 − (8 + 2t) = 12 − 2t, which stays ≥ 0 on [0, 6] (it reaches 0 exactly at t = 6). Profit = ∫₀⁶ (12 − 2t) dt = (12t − t²) |₀⁶ = 72 − 36 = 36. (Check: derivative of 12t − t² is 12 − 2t ✓.) Answer: $36 profit.
10. CHALLENGE: area enclosed by y = x² and y = 2x + 3.
Work: crossings: x² = 2x + 3 → x² − 2x − 3 = 0 → (x − 3)(x + 1) = 0 → x = −1 and x = 3. Top test at x = 0: line → 3, parabola → 0 → line on top. ∫₋₁³ (2x + 3 − x²) dx = (x² + 3x − x³/3) |₋₁³. At 3: 9 + 9 − 9 = 9. At −1: 1 − 3 + 1/3 = −5/3. Area = 9 − (−5/3) = 27/3 + 5/3 = 32/3. (Check: derivative of x² + 3x − x³/3 is 2x + 3 − x² ✓.) Answer: 32/3 square units (≈ 10.67).
