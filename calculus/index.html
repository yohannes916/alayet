Intuitive Integration — a 20-Lesson Course

Intuitive Calculus for a Curious Student · from "rate × time" to u-substitution, one friendly step at a time

🎯 Welcome! This course teaches integral calculus from scratch to a curious student who knows some algebra and has met the idea of a derivative — no other background needed. How to use it: for each lesson, read the notes at the top → do all 20 exercises on paper → reveal the solutions and check yourself → then take the printed test. The printable tests and answer keys live in the same folder as LessonNN_Test.pdf / LessonNN_Answers.pdf. Take your time — every lesson builds on the one before it.

#LessonWhat you'll learn

2Area Under a Graph = the TotalTotals become areas — rectangles, triangles, and trapezoids do the work.

Part 2 The Machinery (Lessons 4–8)

#LessonWhat you'll learn

7The Fundamental Theorem of CalculusThe great shortcut: the exact area is just F(b) − F(a). No slicing needed.
8FTC Practice: Polynomial FluencyDrill the shortcut until definite integrals of polynomials feel automatic.

Part 3 Applications (Lessons 9–14)

#LessonWhat you'll learn
