      Physical Properties
      • Characteristics that are directly observable and unique to

      • Examples
                − Odor

                − Volume
                − State
                − Density

      Section 3.2
      Physical and Chemical Properties and Changes

      Chemical Properties
      • A substance’s ability to form new substances
      • The characteristics that determine how the composition
        of matter changes as a result of contact with other matter
        or the influence of energy
      • Characteristics that describe the behavior of matter
      • Examples
                − Flammability
                − Rusting of steel
                − The digestion of food

      Elements and Compounds

      Element
        •           A substance that cannot be broken down into other
                    substances by chemical methods
        •           Examples
                  − Iron, Fe; aluminum, Al; oxygen, O2; and hydrogen, H2
        •           All of the matter in the world around us contains
                    elements

      Section 3.3
      Elements and Compounds

      Compound
      • A substance composed of a given combination of
        elements that can be broken down into those elements
        by chemical methods
      • Examples
                − Water, H2O; carbon dioxide, CO2; and table sugar,
                  C12H22O11
      • A compound always contains atoms of different
        elements
      • A compound always has the same composition or the
        same combination of atoms
