                                  What is a Solution?  
                                   Solution        is a homogeneous mixture   
                Solvent        is a substance present in the largest amount in a solution  
                Solute        is a substance that dissolves in a solvent to form a solution     
                Aqueous solution        is a solution with water as the solvent    
              
                                  Various Types of Solutions   
                                            
                                  Solubility of Ionic Substances   
                         Ionic substances break up into individual cations and anions when dissolved in water  
                                            
                                  Solubility of Ionic Substances      (continued 1)  
                         Polar water molecules interact with the positive and negative ions of a salt   
                                            
                                  Solubility of Ionic Substances      (continued 2)   
                         Ethanol is soluble in water because of its polar O—H bond   
                                            
                                  Solubility of Polar Substances  
                    Why is solid sugar soluble in water?   
                                            
                                  Substances Insoluble in Water   
                         Nonpolar oil does not interact with polar water  
    Water–water hydrogen bonds keep the water from mixing with the nonpolar molecules   
                                            
                                  How Substances Dissolve  
                         A      “     hole     ”      must be made in the water structure for each solute particle   
    The lost water–water interactions must be replaced by water–solute interactions   
    “     L     ike dissolves like     ”

                                  Terms Associated with the Concentration of a        Solution  
                         The solubility of a solute is limited   
             Saturated solution      contains as much solute as will dissolve at that temperature   
             Unsaturated solution      is a solution that has not reached the limit of solute that will dissolve in it  
              
                                  Terms Associated with the Concentration of a        Solution      (continued)   
                         Solutions are mixtures   
    Amounts of substances can vary in different solutions   
     Specify the amounts of solvent and solute   
     Use qualitative measures of concentration  
         Relatively large amount of solute is dissolved in a          concentrated      solution   
         Relatively small amount of solute is dissolved in a          dilute      solution

                          For a 0.25      M      CaCl     2      solution:  
        		CaCl     2             → Ca       2+        + 2Cl       –

                                  Standard Solution  
                         A solution whose concentration is accurately known  
              
                                  Steps to Make a Standard Solution  
                             Weigh out a sample of solute  
        Transfer it to a volumetric flask  
        Add enough solvent to bring the volume up to the mark on the neck of the flask  
                                            
                                  Dilution  
                         The process of adding water to a concentrated or stock solution to achieve a solution of desired concentration  
    Dilution with water does not alter the numbers of moles of solute present  
    Moles of solute before dilution equals moles of solute after dilution  
        				         M         1         V         1          =          M         2         V         2  
              
                                  Steps to Dilute a Solution  
                               Transfer a measured amount of original solution to a flask containing some water   
          Add water to the flask by swirling to bring the volume up to the calibration mark  
          Mix by inverting the flask

                                  Steps for Solving Stoichiometric Problems Involving Solutions  
                              
        Write the balanced equation for the reaction   
     For reactions involving ions, it is best to write the net ionic equation  
        Calculate the moles of reactants  
        Determine which reactant is limiting  
        Calculate the moles of other reactants or products, as required  
        Convert to grams or other units, if required

                                  Unit of Concentration  
                                   One equivalent of an acid        is the amount of that acid that furnishes 1        mol        of H       +        ions   
              One equivalent of a base        is the amount of that base that furnishes 1        mol        of OH                 ions  
              Equivalent weight                  of an acid or base        is the mass in grams of 1 equivalent of acid or base
