        The Scientific Method
Recognize the problem and state it clearly
◦Make an observation
Propose possible solutions to the problem or possible
explanations for the observation
◦Formulate a hypothesis
Decide which of the solutions is the best, or decide
whether the explanation proposed is reasonable
◦Perform an experiment

           Mass and Weight
Mass – measurement of the amount of matter
in an object.
◦Property of matter that leads to gravitational
 attraction between objects
Weight – measurement of the force of
gravitational attraction of an object and a
significantly larger body.
